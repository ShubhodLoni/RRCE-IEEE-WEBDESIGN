03 Succession

Annual Elections & Leadership Pathways

The IEEE Student Branch conducts annual executive committee transitions in accordance with IEEE student branch guidelines. Active student members from CSE, ISE, ECE, EEE, ME, and allied departments are invited to serve in leadership capacities.

Serving as a branch officer builds crucial project management, public speaking, budgeting, and team leadership competencies.
