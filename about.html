03 Heritage

RajaRajeswari College of Engineering (RRCE)

Verified institutional facts. Established in 2006, RRCE is a premier technological campus situated along the Bengaluru-Mysuru tech corridor.

Autonomous Institute

Autonomous institute under Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

Quality & Trust

ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality engineering education. Proudly sponsored and managed by the Moogambigai Charitable and Educational Trust.

Academic Breadth

Offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across 9 departments: CSE, ISE, ECE, EEE, ME, Robotics & Automation, CSE (IoT, Cyber Security incl. Blockchain), MCA, and MBA.
