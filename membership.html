02 Advantages

Why Become an IEEE Member?

Student membership provides tangible resources that accelerate both academic and industry trajectories.

Publications

IEEE Xplore Access

Access millions of peer-reviewed journal papers, transactions, and technical conference proceedings to supercharge your project work and literature surveys.

Championships

IEEEXtreme & Hackathons

Eligible to participate in the prestigious 24-hour virtual coding contest, student design challenges, and regional paper contests.

Networking

Global Engineering Reach

Connect with engineers, researchers, and IEEE Fellows spanning over 160 countries. Attend international conferences at heavily discounted student rates.

Career

Leadership & Mentorship

Gain leadership experience organizing technical symposiums, holding committee offices, and interacting directly with senior industry delegates.

03 Process

How to Join IEEE in 4 Easy Steps

Official IEEE memberships are processed exclusively through ieee.org. This website does NOT collect membership fees or process passwords.

1

Create IEEE Account

Go to ieee.org and register for a free IEEE web account using your college or personal email address.

2

Select Student Membership

Choose Student Membership. Students in India benefit from special subsidized developing nation / student pricing.

3

Choose RRCE Student Branch

During institutional enrollment, enter RajaRajeswari College of Engineering (RRCE) as your university to link your branch roster.

4

Complete & Connect

Pay dues on ieee.org, receive your IEEE Member ID, and notify our branch coordinator to be added to chapter groups.
